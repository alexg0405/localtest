# Interviewer guide: Amazon behavioral round

Spoilers: this file says what the questions probe. Keep it out of the
candidate's workspace.

## What this round simulates

Amazon interviewers spend much of every round on Leadership Principles stories
and probe each one with follow-ups. Each session picks four different principles
and one question per principle from the shared bank
(`interviews/_amazon/leadership_principles.json`), preferring questions the
candidate hasn't been asked recently. The bank holds 32 questions, two per
principle, each with three follow-ups.

## What strong answers have

- A real, specific situation, not a hypothetical or "I usually…".
- The candidate's own actions, with "I" more than "we".
- A result with a number, and what they learned.
- Follow-up answers that add new detail rather than repeating the story.
- The principle itself visible in what they did (each bank entry lists its
  signals and what to look for).

## Follow-up questions to ask

- What would you do differently if it happened again tomorrow?
- Which of your stories best shows how you handle a deadline?

## Signals

Green: concise stories in about 2–3 minutes; numbers; owns mistakes; asks
thoughtful questions at the end.
Red: hypotheticals; "we" throughout; no result; blames others; the same story
for every question.
