# Scoring rubric: résumé deep dive

Score each area 1–5, or "not observed" when there is no evidence.

| Area | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Background walkthrough | Rambling or one line. | A clear summary of a few items. | Two to three minutes: where they are, the experiences that matter and why, what they want next. |
| Technical depth | Can't explain how their projects work. | Explains the main pieces. | Explains the architecture, a key decision with the alternatives, and the hardest bug, in specifics. |
| Ownership and impact | "We" throughout, no results. | Some "I" and one result. | Clear personal ownership and a number for each result. |
| Handling follow-ups | Dodges or repeats. | Answers most follow-ups. | Every follow-up gets a direct answer with new detail. |
| Teamwork and judgment | No example. | An example without a clear outcome. | A specific STAR story with what they did and what they learned. |
| Communication and questions | Monologues or one-liners; no questions. | Mostly clear. | Concise answers of a minute or two, and thoughtful questions at the end. |
