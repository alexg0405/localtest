# Scoring rubric: Palantir FDSE learning interview

Score each area 1–5, or "not observed" when there is no evidence. This round
is about learning, not about knowing: finishing every part matters less than
how the candidate got to working code.

| Area | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reading and understanding | Codes before reading; guesses relay's behavior. | Gets the main ideas from the docs, and explains most of what they use correctly. | Explains relay's ideas and the sync's flow correctly as they use them, and checks the docs again when unsure. |
| Questions | None, or only ones the docs answer. | A few relevant questions. | Few, purposeful questions, including the real ambiguity (other errors), with nothing the docs already answer. |
| Applying the library | Part 1 doesn't work. | Parts 1 and 2 work. | All parts work, using relay as documented (keys, gather, Retry, cancellation, callbacks). |
| Pace through the parts | Stuck on part 1. | Part 1 working and tested in the 25 minutes. | Three or four parts, each tested before moving on (two is a solid 4). |
| Code quality | Hard to follow. | Clear. | Small functions, clear names, no duplicated paging logic. |
| Communication and honesty | Silent, or bluffs. | Explains when asked. | Narrates the mental model, says "I'm not sure" when they aren't, and asks good questions. |
