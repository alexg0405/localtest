# Scoring rubric: Amazon behavioral round

Score each area 1–5, or "not observed" when there is no evidence.

| Area | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leadership Principles | The principle isn't visible. | Partly visible in some stories. | Clearly shown in every story. |
| Story structure (STAR) | Rambling, no result. | Most parts present. | Every story has all four parts in a clear order. |
| Ownership, specifics, and impact | "We", no numbers. | Some personal actions or numbers. | "I" throughout, with a measurable result each time. |
| Handling follow-ups | Deflects or repeats. | Answers most of them. | Adds new, specific detail to every follow-up. |
| Self-awareness and learning | No lessons. | Some reflection. | Owns mistakes and names what changed. |
| Communication and pacing | Too short or far too long. | Mostly 2–3 minutes. | Concise, well paced, with good questions at the end. |
