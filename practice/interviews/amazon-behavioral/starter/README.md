# Amazon behavioral round (practice)

Amazon interviewers spend a large part of every round on its 16 Leadership
Principles, and they dig into each story with follow-ups. This round practices
only that.

## How this round works

| Minutes | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | Introductions. The interviewer asks you to introduce yourself. |
| 2–40 | Four Leadership Principles questions, a different mix every session, each with three follow-ups. |
| 40–45 | Your questions for the interviewer. |

- Press the mic in the Interviewer tab and answer out loud, as you would in the
  real interview, or type your answers in the chat. When you pause, the
  interviewer asks its next follow-up.
- Use STAR: the Situation and Task in a sentence or two, most of your time on
  your Actions, then the Result, with a number if you can, and what you
  learned.
- Say "I", not "we". The interviewer wants to know what you did.
- Aim for about 2–3 minutes per story before the follow-ups.
- Use `stories.md` to jot notes or keep your story bank.
- No AI.

## The 16 Leadership Principles

Customer Obsession · Ownership · Invent and Simplify · Are Right, A Lot ·
Learn and Be Curious · Hire and Develop the Best · Insist on the Highest
Standards · Think Big · Bias for Action · Frugality · Earn Trust · Dive Deep ·
Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit · Deliver Results · Strive to be Earth's Best
Employer · Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility

Read Amazon's own description of each one on amazon.jobs.
